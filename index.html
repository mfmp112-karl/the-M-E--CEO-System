The framework

What an M-E CEO actually does differently.

Every business needs three kinds of thinking to grow. Most owners collapse all three into one frantic blur. The M-E CEO separates them — and never works without knowing which mode they are in.

E
Entrepreneur
"What problem do I solve next?"
Designs the future. Builds the offer. Spots the next opportunity.
M
Manager
"What system should be running this?"
Builds processes. Tracks numbers. Turns decisions into routines.
T
Technician
"What do I finish today?"
Executes the task. Serves the customer. Most owners live here permanently — and that is the trap.
Before Karl opens his laptop for any working session, he states which mode he is in and what he is trying to produce. If you cannot name the mode — you are drifting.
